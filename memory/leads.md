# Sales leads

Optional. The sales pipeline. One section per lead, with fixed fields.

- **Stages:** `lead`, `intro`, `demo`, `pilot`, `negotiation`, `won`, `lost`.
- **Last contact:** the date and the channel of the last message or meeting.
- The morning summary flags a lead with no contact for 7 days or more, a
  next step date that is today or past, and a meeting today.
